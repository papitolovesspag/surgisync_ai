# SurgiSync (Predictive Surgical Readiness & Sterile Supply)

> **Enterprise Operational Intelligence Engine Linking EHR Schedules, Computer Vision Sterile Processing Auditing, and Dynamic Inventory Optimization.**

SurgiSync bridges the high-stakes gap between the Operating Room (OR) and the Sterile Processing Department (SPD). Surgeries are frequently delayed or cancelled due to missing or delayed instrument trays, while health systems lose $3M–$5M in expired single-use supplies annually. SurgiSync eliminates these bottlenecks with real-time operational AI.

---

## Key Capabilities

1. **Multimodal Tray Vision Auditing**
   - Overhead computer vision cameras at sterile pack assembly stations visually cross-check 100+ surgical instruments against master CAD specifications in under 3 seconds.
   - Detects missing clamps, broken blades, and micro-defects before trays are sealed and autoclaved.

2. **Dynamic SPD Turnaround & Autoclave Queue Prediction**
   - Multi-agent simulation continuously models wash, ultrasonic, autoclave, and cool-down workflows in sync with dynamic EHR surgical schedules.
   - Automatically prioritizes urgent or rescheduled cases to prevent OR idle time.

3. **Reinforcement Learning Preference Card Optimization**
   - Dynamically trims over-ordered implants, specialty sutures, and single-use supplies without compromising clinical readiness.
   - Recovers hundreds of thousands of dollars in annual surgical scrap per hospital.

4. **Clinical IT & Governance Interoperability**
   - Direct bi-directional integration with Epic Systems (FHIR R4) and Oracle Health / Cerner.
   - HIPAA compliant, SOC 2 Type II governance, and on-premises edge inference for data sovereignty.
